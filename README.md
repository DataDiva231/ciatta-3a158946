# Ciatta

Build ciatta mvp. Ciatta is a continuous women's health intelligence system that learns from your health data, lifestyle, and environment to build a personalized understanding of your body over time. Powered by a proprietary machine learning platform, a smart sensing earring, and an intelligent honeycomb-structured tampon, Ciatta transforms everyday signals into continuous health understanding, delivering personalized insights, emerging patterns, and guidance for every stage of a woman's life. the visually style is inspired by the mix of coco health and olivia health the the tone of voice is inspired by rox, https://www.oliviahealth.ai/, https://www.cocohealth.com/, and https://www.talktorox.com/
